# Boomer Automation Website

This repository contains the official marketing website for Boomer Automation. It is designed to present the company’s services, capabilities, and value proposition through a modern, responsive, and performance-focused interface.

---

## Overview

Boomer Automation is a digital solutions provider specializing in web development, search optimization, paid advertising, and business automation. The website serves as a centralized platform to communicate these offerings with clarity, consistency, and a strong emphasis on conversion.

The project reflects a focus on clean design, intuitive navigation, and structured content that aligns with real-world client acquisition and growth strategies.

---

## Capabilities Highlighted

- Custom website development, including both WordPress-based solutions for ease of management and fully custom builds for advanced functionality and design
- SEO optimization and Google Ads campaign management to drive targeted traffic and measurable growth
- Lead generation systems designed to capture, track, and convert users efficiently
- Business automation workflows to streamline operations and reduce manual overhead
- Scalable hosting solutions, including managed hosting and client-owned environments

---

## Key Features

- Fully responsive layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices  
- Clean, modern UI with consistent visual hierarchy  
- Scroll-based reveal animations to enhance user engagement  
- Modular structure with reusable components  
- Dedicated sections for services, process, pricing, and company overview  
- Conversion-focused design with clear calls to action  

---

## Technology Stack

- HTML5  
- CSS3  
- Bootstrap 5  
- Vanilla JavaScript  

---

## Design Approach

The site is built around a dark, modern aesthetic with subtle gradients, glass-style surfaces, and refined motion. A black and gold treatment is applied to key structural elements such as the header and footer to reinforce a premium brand identity.

Attention is given to spacing, typography, and interaction feedback to ensure a cohesive and professional presentation throughout.
