Working rhythm

What happens at every stage.

The table makes the working relationship explicit: what happens, what the client provides, what Boomer Automation handles, and what should exist before the project moves forward.

After launch

Defined scope. Ongoing feedback. No open-ended guessing.

Support depends on the selected package: 14 days for One-Page Starter, 30 days for Professional Launch, 60 days for Business Growth, and 90 days for Premium Digital Presence.

01

What counts

Launch-related technical defects and work directly connected to the delivered scope.

02

What does not

New pages, new features, major copy changes, or platform management outside the agreed project.

03

Launch is not the finish line

Future SEO, ads, maintenance, content, or workflow improvements can be scoped separately.
