1. Overview

Boomer Automation Inc. respects your privacy. We collect personal information only where it is reasonably needed to operate our website, respond to inquiries, provide services, improve our marketing, track performance, protect our systems, and communicate with potential or existing clients.

By using this website, submitting a form, contacting us, or choosing to work with us, you acknowledge that your information may be collected and used as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your business or company name
  • Your website URL, if provided
  • Your service interests or project requirements
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Information shared through email, phone, text, or other business communications

We may also collect limited technical and usage information when you visit the website, such as device type, browser type, pages viewed, approximate location, referring pages, general traffic source, and interactions with buttons, forms, or links.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests
  • Understand your business needs and project goals
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, recommendations, or follow-up messages
  • Provide website, SEO, Google Ads, automation, tracking, or consulting services
  • Improve our website, user experience, content, and service offerings
  • Measure advertising, SEO, and conversion performance
  • Track which marketing channels generate inquiries
  • Manage project communication and business records
  • Detect, prevent, or respond to spam, abuse, fraud, or security issues

We do not sell personal information.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Conversion Tracking

We may use analytics, advertising, and measurement tools such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, or similar services to better understand how visitors find and use our website.

These tools may help us measure page views, traffic sources, button clicks, form activity, phone link clicks, advertising performance, and other conversion-related actions.

These services may use cookies, tags, pixels, device information, browser information, and similar technologies to provide measurement and reporting. This helps us improve our website, marketing campaigns, lead generation process, and service experience.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at: Google’s partner sites information page .

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party tools may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, or similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics, advertising measurement, spam prevention, and performance monitoring.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the website or certain measurement features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

6. Forms, Email, Phone, and Business Communications

When you submit a form, email us, call us, or otherwise communicate with Boomer Automation Inc., we may use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project, provide service recommendations, and follow up with relevant business communication.

Inquiry details may also be collected, stored, and managed in CRM or lead-management tools so we can organize leads, track follow-up, maintain business records, manage project communication, and improve our response process.

If you become a client, we may use your information for onboarding, project communication, service delivery, billing coordination, reporting, maintenance, support, and related business purposes.

If we send commercial electronic messages, we aim to include a reasonable way to unsubscribe or ask us to stop sending non-essential marketing communications.

7. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website, manage forms, host files, process communications, measure analytics, run advertising, manage leads, support automation, or deliver services.

These providers may include hosting platforms, form handling services, analytics tools, advertising platforms, email providers, CRM tools, automation platforms, project management tools, payment or billing tools, and other technical vendors.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to comply with legal obligations, to enforce our rights, to protect our systems, or to investigate suspected fraud, abuse, spam, or security issues.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. This may include responding to inquiries, providing services, maintaining business records, improving our website, resolving disputes, supporting security, and meeting legal or operational requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably needed, we will delete, anonymize, or securely retain it according to appropriate business and legal requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No website, form, email system, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting sensitive information through website forms unless it is necessary.

10. Your Choices and Access Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable legal, security, and business record requirements.

You may also ask us to stop sending non-essential marketing messages or stop using your information for certain non-essential purposes where applicable.

12. Children’s Privacy
