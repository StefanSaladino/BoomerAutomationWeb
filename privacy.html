Boomer Automation Inc. Privacy Policy

This policy applies when you visit boomerautomation.com, submit a form, contact us, or communicate about our services.

Overview

Boomer Automation Inc. respects your privacy. We collect personal information only where reasonably needed to operate our website, respond to inquiries, provide services, improve our marketing, track performance, protect our systems, and communicate with potential or existing clients.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, business name, website URL, service interests, project requirements, and details submitted through forms, email, phone, text, or other business communication.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, understand business needs, prepare proposals or recommendations, deliver agreed services, improve the website, measure marketing performance, manage project communication, maintain business records, and prevent spam, abuse, fraud, or security issues. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics, Advertising, and Conversion Tracking

We may use tools such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Search Console, or similar services to understand traffic sources, page views, button clicks, form activity, phone-link clicks, advertising performance, and other conversion actions. Optional measurement should follow the privacy choice presented on this website.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website and third-party services may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, or similar technology for functionality, analytics, advertising measurement, spam prevention, and performance monitoring. Browser settings can be used to control or disable cookies.

Forms and Business Communications

When you submit a form, email us, call us, or otherwise communicate with Boomer Automation Inc., we may use the information to respond, discuss your project, provide recommendations, and follow up. Inquiry details may be organized in CRM or lead-management tools for follow-up, project communication, reporting, and business records. This does not mean a proprietary Boomer CRM product is being offered to clients.

Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to host the website, manage forms, process communication, measure analytics, run advertising, organize leads, support workflows, process billing, or deliver services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights, systems, or users.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for inquiry response, service delivery, business records, security, dispute resolution, and legal or operational requirements. Information no longer needed will be deleted, anonymized, or securely retained according to appropriate requirements.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No website, form, email system, or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information through website forms.

Your Rights and Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable legal, security, and business-record requirements. You may also ask us to stop non-essential marketing messages or certain non-essential uses where applicable.

Third-Party Services

This website may link to third-party websites or use third-party services. Boomer Automation Inc. is not responsible for their privacy practices, content, security, or policies. Review the applicable third-party policy before providing information.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the website, tools, services, legal obligations, or business operations. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
