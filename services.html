Platform guidance

The platform should serve the business, not complicate it.

Custom-coded website

Recommended for most service-business marketing sites.

  • Faster, lighter, and more secure
  • Built around exact goals
  • Fewer updates and plugins
  • Low routine maintenance

WordPress

Useful when frequent client-managed publishing is genuinely needed.

  • Easy content and page updates
  • Large ecosystem
  • Good for content-heavy sites
  • Requires ongoing maintenance

E-commerce

Quoted separately, starting at $3,500.

  • Products, cart, checkout, and payments
  • Inventory and order systems
  • Shipping, tax, and policies
  • Usually Shopify or WooCommerce
