WHAT BASIC IMAGE PREPARATION MEANS

Cropping, resizing, compression, file-format conversion, and minor brightness or contrast adjustment. Background removal, object removal,
detailed retouching, reconstruction, and composite work are advanced image manipulation.

WHAT COPY EDITING MEANS

Improving headings, clarity, structure, and calls to action using client-supplied information. Writing the website from a blank page, conducting
interviews, or producing research-heavy content is full copywriting.

All add-ons are confirmed in writing before work begins. No CRM integration, booking integration, or additional-language
implementation is included in the current package framework.
